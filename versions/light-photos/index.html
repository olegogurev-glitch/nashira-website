The Academy

A private-club atmosphere with an academy's discipline — tennis the way it is played at Europe's great clay clubs.

Nashira is a tennis academy and club close to Konyaaltı beach and the city centre. Our six red clay courts are the slowest, most forgiving surface in the game — the place where patience, footwork and shot-making are learned properly.

Whether you are picking up a racket for the first time, raising a junior with ambitions, or looking for a regular game in Antalya, our coaches build the programme around you.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need to be a member to play?

No. Courts and lessons are open to everyone. Members simply enjoy priority booking, unlimited daytime play and discounts on coaching.

I'm visiting Antalya — can I play for a few days?

Of course. Book a court online, take a lesson, or join a three- or five-day tennis holiday camp. Rackets and balls are available to rent.

Which languages do your coaches speak?

Our team coaches in Turkish, English and Russian.

What should I wear on clay courts?

Tennis shoes with a clay or all-court (herringbone) sole. Running shoes and dark-soled trainers damage the surface, so please avoid them.

From what age can children start?

From four years old. Our Junior Academy uses softer red, orange and green balls and smaller courts so children rally from the very first lesson.

How do cancellations work?

Court bookings and lessons can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time.
