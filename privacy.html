We process your data because it is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you (the booking or lesson, KVKK Art. 5/2(c)) and for our legitimate interests (Art. 5/2(f)). Where the enquiry form asks for your agreement to be contacted, that is based on your explicit consent (Art. 5/1).

  • WhatsApp: booking and enquiry requests reach us as a WhatsApp message that you send. WhatsApp is provided by WhatsApp LLC / Meta, and data may be processed on servers outside Türkiye (KVKK Art. 9). [Legal basis for transfer abroad to be confirmed by a legal adviser.]

Partly automatically: the details you enter in the website forms are turned into a WhatsApp message, which you then choose to send to us.

7. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your request and for any period required by law; after that it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised. [The club will set a specific retention period.]

8. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed and to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties it is transferred to; to have incomplete or incorrect data corrected; to have it deleted or destroyed under the conditions of Art. 7 and to have these actions notified to third parties; to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

9. Contacting us

Send requests about your rights in writing to the address above or to [club email address]. We answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest, as required by the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller.
